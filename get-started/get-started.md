## Introduction to LifeTech OCMS

LifeTech OCMS is a PHP-based modular content management system and application framework for building websites, portals, APIs and business applications. It combines content management with developer-oriented application architecture.

## Core features

1. MVC architecture with models, controllers, services and views.
1. Modular packages: themes for presentation, modules for functionality and plugins for extensions.
1. DDM (Data Defacing Model), including framework-specific dynamic content loading.
1. LW-Token authentication and role-based access control.
1. Maplite for database-query chaining and data access.
1. JLON for client-side data representation.
1. LifeRazor for templating.
1. REST APIs, routing and request/response utilities.
1. Frontend flexibility with PHP and JavaScript, including React and Vue integrations.
1. Theme components, views, assets and dynamic content loading.

## Origins and dedication

LifeTech OCMS originated in the Computer Science Department of Ogun State Institute of Technology (OGITECH), Igbesa. It was innovated by Ajayi Abolore Abdulgafaru and is dedicated to the memory of the late Mr. Aluko Temitope, a lecturer at OGITECH.

## Vision, licensing and security

Vision: to make LifeTech OCMS a globally adopted, secure, flexible and developer-friendly platform for web applications, enterprise solutions and e-commerce.

Licence: LifeTech OCMS is described as open-source software under the MIT License. Check the licence supplied with your downloaded release for its authoritative terms.

Security reports: send suspected vulnerabilities privately to **abolorea@lifetech.host**. Avoid publicly disclosing exploit details before maintainers can investigate.

## What can you build?

Examples include business websites, blogs, learning portals, dashboards, content-rich websites and custom API-backed applications. Available capabilities depend on the installed version and packages.
