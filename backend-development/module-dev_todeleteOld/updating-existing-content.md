Existing content can be updated by uploading a new version or edit content details 

**Steps:**

1. Select the content
1. Click **Upload Content for Update** 
1. Upload the updated content file.
1. Save changes
1. OR, click the **Edit** (pencil) icon under the Actions column to modify its internal settings.

**Result:**

 The content is updated while keeping its original reference.
