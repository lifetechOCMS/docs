# Publish to Content

[Previous](adding-a-new-page.md) · [Main documentation](../../README.md) · [Guide contents](README.md) · [Next](toggle-module-route.md)

This action allows you to link the current page to specific content components. **Steps:**

1. Locate the desired page in the Life Page list.
1. Click on any page in **Page Name** column.
1. The system will open the publish page to content dialog box.
1. Select the necessary content elements you wish to display on this page and confirm your selection.
