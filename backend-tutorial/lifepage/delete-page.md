# Delete Page

[Previous](view-page-details.md) · [Main documentation](../../README.md) · [Guide contents](README.md) · Next

This action permanently removes the page and its associated routing from the system. **Steps:**

1. Select the page you wish to remove from the list.
1. Click the **Delete Page** action icon. 
1. Confirm the deletion when prompted by the system.

**Warning:** Deleting a page is permanent. It will immediately remove the page from the front end and may result in broken links if the page is still referenced in your navigation menus or other content.

Source: https://www.lifetech.host/hubs/Docs#delete-page
