# Life Media

[Previous](accessing-the-file-manager.md) · [Main documentation](../../README.md) · [Guide contents](README.md) · Next

The "Life Media" tab is a dedicated, simplified space specifically for managing your website's most important branding images.

Here, you manage two specific items:

1. **Logo:** The main graphic that represents your website.
1. **Favicon:** The tiny icon that appears on the top tab of your web browser next to your website's name.

**How it works:** To keep things organized, the system ensures that there is only ever *one* active logo and *one* active favicon at a time.

- **Uploading:** If the space is currently empty, clicking the blue button will prompt you to upload your image.
- **Replacing:** If an image is already there, the button will say **Change Logo** or **Change Favicon**. Clicking it will let you upload a new image, which will automatically overwrite and replace the old one across your entire website.
