# Accessing Life Content

[Previous](life-content.md) · [Main documentation](../../README.md) · [Guide contents](README.md) · [Next](adding-new-content.md)

Steps:

1. From the left sidebar navigation, expand the Content section, click Life Content
1. The Life Content workspace opens

 ![Accessing Life Content screenshot](https://www.lifetech.host/hubs/packages/modules/MdDocs/Media/3acb12aec887a59de2ba367c961bc989.png)

The Life Content screen provides tools to:

- Add new content
- Preview content before publishing
- Publish content to one or more pages
- Update existing content
- Deleting Content
