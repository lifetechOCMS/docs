# Adding a New Role

[Previous](life-user-role.md) · [Main documentation](../../README.md) · [Guide contents](README.md) · [Next](managing-existing-roles.md)

**Steps:**

1. From the left sidebar navigation, expand the **Users** section.
1. Click on **Life User Role**.
1. Click the button to add a new role. 
1. The **Add New Role** window will appear.
1. Enter the **Role Name**.
1. Select the **Status** (e.g., Unprotected).
1. Click **Add** to create the new role group.
