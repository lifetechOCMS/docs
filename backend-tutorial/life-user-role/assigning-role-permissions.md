# Assigning Role Permissions

[Previous](managing-existing-roles.md) · [Main documentation](../../README.md) · [Guide contents](README.md) · Next

Clicking the Permission button allows you to grant privileges to pages or routers for that specific role. Steps:Locate the role and click its Permission button.The Publish Role dialog box will open.Navigate through the available tabs to assign access to Packages, Router, or Contents.Check the box next to specific items (like mdBook or mdLtTheme) to grant access.For bulk actions, use the Grant Privilege to all or Revoke all Privilege buttons at the top of the dialog box.

Source: https://www.lifetech.host/hubs/Docs#assigning-role-permissions
